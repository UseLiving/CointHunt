Words you'll hear

The games borrow real crypto slang. Here's what it means.

Candle
One bar on a price chart. Green means the price rose over that period, red means it fell. The thin lines show the highest and lowest prices.
Rug pull
When the people behind a crypto coin drain the money and disappear. It's one of the most common scams, which is why Rug Sweeper exists.
HODL
Holding a coin through the ups and downs instead of selling. It started as a typo in a forum post and turned into a slogan.
Moon
When a price shoots up. Crash games borrow the idea: the multiplier climbs, then it stops at a random moment.
Bull, bear
A bull market is rising and a bear market is falling. Traders try to read the trend, and trends can flip without warning.
Wallet
An app that holds the keys to your crypto. Never share your recovery phrase with anyone, or type it into a website.

Rules and questions

Weekly prize rules
  1. Free to enter. You never pay anything to play or to be eligible. Coins have no cash value and can't be cashed out.
  2. One prize a week. Each week runs from Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. When it ends, the player with the most points on the "Overall, this week" board wins 50 SOL. A tie splits the prize equally.
  3. How points work. Each of the six skill games (Candle Catcher, Rug Sweeper, Block Merge, Wallet Match, Chain Snake, Bull or Bear) gives up to 100 points for your best score that week, so the most you can earn is 600. Moon Crash and HODL Clicker don't count. Every player has the same abilities in every game.
  4. Fair play. Scores come from your browser and are checked by hand before any prize is sent. Scores that look impossible, automated or edited are removed, and that player can be disqualified.
  5. Getting paid. The winner is announced on this page and on X (@PlayCoinHunt), and we arrange payment with them directly, in SOL to an address they give us. We post the payment transaction publicly so anyone can check it. We will never ask for a recovery phrase, never ask you to sign anything, and never ask you to pay anything to receive a prize.
  6. Eligibility. You must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live. The prize is void where it isn't allowed, and you're responsible for any taxes and for following the laws where you live.
  7. Optional contributions. You can contribute SOL to the prize wallet and receive a skin and bonus coins as a thank-you. Contributions are voluntary, non-refundable and give no advantage in the prize: it stays free to enter, and the leaderboard only counts game scores.
  8. Changes. The prize is a separate promotion run by the CoinHunt team. We can change, pause or end it, and we'll update this page when we do.
Are the coins real crypto?

No. Coins are play money that only exist on this page. They can't be sold, sent or withdrawn. Contributors to the prize wallet get bonus coins as a thank-you. The weekly prize is paid separately, in SOL, by the CoinHunt team.

What do I get for contributing to the pot?

Three tiers: 0.05 SOL gives the Gold Hunter skin and 500 coins, 0.25 SOL gives Emerald Hunter and 3,000 coins, and 1 SOL gives Obsidian Hunter and 15,000 coins. Skins change how Coiny, Pouch, Sir Hiss and the clicker coin look, and add a star next to your name on the leaderboard. Contributions are voluntary and non-refundable, and they never improve your chances in the prize. To claim, send from your own wallet, connect that wallet here, paste the transaction signature and press Verify. We check the payment on the Solana blockchain before unlocking anything.

Why does claiming say it cannot reach the network?

Verification needs to read the Solana blockchain. Some hosts block outside connections. On those, you can still see the wallet address and play, but claims will not work until the site is opened from its own address.

What do abilities do?

Every game has a few abilities you can trigger, like a magnet in Candle Catcher or undo in Block Merge. Some are free and recharge, and some cost a little of your winnings. They're the same for everyone.

What does connecting a wallet do?

It shows your public address on this page and nothing else. CoinHunt never asks you to sign a message or approve a transaction, and it can't move your funds. You don't need a wallet to play or to enter the prize.

Can I win real money?

Coins can never be turned into money. The only real prize is the weekly 50 SOL for the top player on the leaderboard, and it costs nothing to enter (see the rules). Anything that asks you to send money or share a recovery phrase to "play" or "claim" is not this site. Be careful with real crypto games: the operator sets the odds and the payouts, and many are built to take your money.

Why can't I post a score?

The shared leaderboard is only available to people the platform signs in. Everyone else can still play, and their scores show on their own device.

Is Moon Crash gambling?

It copies how crash gambling games work, using play coins. It has a small house edge on purpose, like the real ones, so over many rounds you'll lose coins. Real crash games do the same with real money, which is the reason to try it here first.

Where is my progress saved?

In this browser, on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it.
